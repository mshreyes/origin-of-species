# On the Origin of Species — Enhanced Reader

An interactive web version of Charles Darwin's *On the Origin of Species* (1859 First Edition), brought to you via Project Gutenberg. 

This project aims to make this classic book easier and more engaging to read by adding helpful study resources, Wikipedia tooltips, and clear modern science notes directly into the pages.

---

## Features

* **Custom Reading Themes & Fonts:** Switch easily between Paper, Light, Sepia, Slate Dark, and OLED Black themes. You can also adjust text size, line widths, and fonts (Serif, Sans-Serif, or Monospace).
* **Smart Concept Links:** Important terms, ideas, and names from Darwin's text are automatically highlighted so you can click them or hover over them for quick pop-up summaries.
* **Wikipedia Lookup:** Hover over any key term for a quick preview, or open the Wikipedia sidebar to read full summaries right alongside the book.
* **Darwin vs. Modern Science Notes:** Includes chapter summaries and notes that explain how modern genetics, DNA, and science have updated what Darwin wrote in 1859.
* **Helpful Reading Resources:** A built-in resource library and bibliography linking to companion books, online archives (like Darwin Online), and interactive evolution tools.
